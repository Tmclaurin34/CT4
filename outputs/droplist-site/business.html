How customers opt in

Consent is collected by this business before any message is sent — in person by scanning a check-in QR code (goclicktide.com/checkin) or completing a sign-up form, where the customer checks an unchecked-by-default consent box. Opt-in is never required to make a purchase, and consent is specific to this business.

By checking a box you agree to the Privacy Policy & Terms. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect. This business collects customer details such as name, phone number, email, mailing address, and visit or purchase history — provided by you or synced from the business's point-of-sale system.

How we use it. To deliver the loyalty, reminder, and marketing messages you opted into, and to operate this business's customer program.

Mobile/SMS information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers and SMS consent records are used solely to deliver the messages described here and are never sold.

Service providers. This business uses Clicktide (operated by Six Seasons Partners LLC) as its messaging platform to deliver these messages. Data shared with Clicktide is processed only to provide the service and is not sold or shared for third-party marketing.

Your choices. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, HELP for help. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting this business.

SMS Program

  • Message types: thank-yous, satisfaction surveys, appointment/visit reminders, win-back check-ins, rewards, and gift/order notifications.
  • Frequency: varies by business and activity, typically 1–4 messages per month.
  • Rates: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt-out: reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe (one confirmation, then no further messages). Reply START to re-subscribe.
  • Help: reply HELP, or contact this business.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
