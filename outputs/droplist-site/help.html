Getting started

What does Clicktide do?

Clicktide watches your customer activity and, when a regular starts slipping away, sends them something from you: an email, a text, a mailed postcard, or a small branded gift. It runs on its own once your campaigns are on.

How long does setup take?

About 10 minutes: pick your business type, create your account, connect your register, choose a gift and your triggers, then go live.

Why didn't anything send right after I connected?

For the first 48 hours after your register connects, Clicktide doesn't send anything. That gives your customer history time to import so nobody gets a message based on incomplete data. After that, campaigns check for matches every hour.

Will my existing customers get blasted on day one?

No. Welcome and milestone campaigns only react to visits that happen after you turn the campaign on. Win-back campaigns look at how long it's been since each customer's last visit, and each campaign has a cooldown so the same person isn't contacted again too soon.

Connecting your register

Which systems can I connect?

Square, Clover and Shopify connect directly. You can also add customers by importing a spreadsheet. More integrations are on the way; email us if you need one.

Can Clicktide change anything in my register?

No. Access is read-only. Clicktide reads customers and visits so it knows who's drifting. It never charges cards, edits orders or changes your settings.

My customers aren't showing up.

Give the first import a few minutes, then refresh. If the list is still empty, disconnect and reconnect from your dashboard, or email support with your business name and we'll check it.

Campaigns

What triggers can I use?
  • Win-back: a customer hasn't been back in a while (Clicktide learns each customer's normal rhythm)
  • Welcome and second-visit nudges for new customers
  • Visit milestones, big spenders and VIPs
  • Anniversaries
Who gets skipped?

Customers who rated their experience poorly are skipped for reward campaigns, people who unsubscribed aren't emailed, and texts only go to people who opted in. Each business also has an hourly send cap as a safety limit.

Can I pause a campaign?

Yes. Open Campaigns in your dashboard and switch it off. Nothing else sends from it until you turn it back on.

Gifts & wallet

How are gifts paid for?

Physical gifts are paid from your gift wallet, which you add funds to from the Wallet page. The cost is taken out when an item is sent, and every charge shows in your wallet history.

What if a customer's mailing address is missing?

Clicktide asks the customer directly with a secure link (by text if they opted in, otherwise email). It only asks for a mailing address, never payment details. Once they fill it in, the address appears on their profile.

Do gifts send automatically?

Only if you turn on gift auto-send. Otherwise each matched gift waits in your dashboard for you to review and send.

Can I get unused wallet funds back?

Yes. Email support and we'll refund your unused balance.

How do I track a gift?

Open Shipments in your dashboard to follow each gift from branded to boxed, shipped and delivered.

Texting

Who can receive texts?

Only customers who have opted in to texts from your business. Clicktide checks consent before every text.

How do customers stop texts?

They can reply STOP at any time and won't get more texts. Replying HELP returns contact info.

Billing & canceling

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 30 days. If you cancel during the trial, you aren't charged.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In your dashboard, tap Manage subscription. From there you can switch plans, update your card, or cancel.

Where are my invoices?

Tap Manage subscription in your dashboard to view and download past invoices.

Your data

Who can see my customer list?

Only you and, when you ask for help, Clicktide support. We never sell your customer data. See our privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Cancel your subscription, then email support@goclicktide.com from your account email and we'll delete your account and customer data.

Email support@goclicktide.com or use the chat button in your dashboard.
