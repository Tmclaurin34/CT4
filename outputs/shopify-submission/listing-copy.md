**App details (474/500)**
> Clicktide reads your Shopify orders (read-only) and learns how often each repeat customer usually buys. It shows who is on schedule, who is overdue compared with their own pattern, and what those customers have spent with you. Turn on win-back campaigns by email, opted-in text, mailed postcard or branded gift. Nothing is sent until you switch a campaign on, and messages stop when a customer orders again. Texts, postcards and gifts are for customers in the United States.

**Features**
1. Learns each repeat customer's usual time between orders
2. Flags customers who are overdue compared with their own pattern
3. Win-back emails, opted-in texts, mailed postcards and branded gifts
4. Nothing is sent until you turn a campaign on
5. Stops a customer's win-back messages when they order again
