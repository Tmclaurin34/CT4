Questions owners ask

Will it send anything without my say-so?

No. Nothing goes out until you turn a campaign on, and you can edit or pause any of them in one click.

Does it change anything in my POS?

No. Clicktide connects read-only. It reads visits and orders to learn each customer's rhythm — it never edits your register, prices or inventory.

Who are the texts and emails from?

From your business, in your name and colors. Texts only go to customers who opted in, only during sensible local hours, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

What do postcards and gifts cost?

Postcards are $1.50 each. Gifts are priced per item and shown before anything is sent. You'll never be surprised by a bill.

How long does setup take?

About 10 minutes: connect your register, pick the win-back plan, and Clicktide imports your history and starts learning that night.

Is my customers' data safe?

Each business can only ever see its own data, connections are encrypted, and two-step login is available for every account. See our privacy policy.
