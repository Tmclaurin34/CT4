**App name:** Clicktide

**Subtitle / tagline:** See which repeat customers stopped reordering, and bring them back.

**Introduction (short description):**
Clicktide learns how often each of your customers normally orders and shows you who has gone quiet, so you can win them back before they're gone for good.

**Detailed description:**
Some customers reorder coffee every few weeks; others buy pet food once a month. A single "inactive after X days" rule treats them all the same, so it either nags loyal customers or notices lost ones too late.

Clicktide reads your Shopify orders (read-only) and learns each customer's own reorder rhythm. Inside your Shopify admin it shows who is on their usual rhythm, who is drifting past it, and how much those customers have spent with you. Open the full Clicktide dashboard for every customer's history and status.

When you're ready, turn on win-back campaigns: a personal email, an opted-in text message, a mailed postcard or a small branded gift. Nothing is sent until you switch a campaign on. Texts only go to customers who opted in, during daytime hours in your time zone. When a customer orders again, Clicktide counts them as back and stops their win-back messages.

**Key benefits (3 bullets):**
- Each customer's own reorder rhythm, not one fixed timer
- See who's drifting right inside your Shopify admin
- Email, opted-in texts, postcards and gifts, with nothing sent until you turn it on
