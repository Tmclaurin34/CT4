Security & privacy

Clicktide only reads your sales data. It never moves money.

You connect through your POS company's own login page, so Clicktide never sees your password. Square, Shopify and Stripe connections ask only for read permissions. With every POS, Clicktide only ever reads orders and customers.

What Clicktide reads

  • Completed orders: date and total
  • The customer on each order (name, email, phone), only to recognize repeat visits
  • Your business name and locations

What Clicktide never does

  • Take payments, issue refunds or move money
  • Change prices, products, inventory or settings
  • Message your customers during the free audit
  • Sell or share your customer data

What happens to the data

  • Your report shows totals only — no customer names, emails or phone numbers
  • Only you can open your report, while signed in
  • The audit keeps no customer contact details: it works from visit counts, totals and dates, then deletes those once your report is built
  • Sent over encrypted connections

Disconnecting takes one click

On your report, Disconnect and delete my data deletes Clicktide's stored access key for your POS, your report, and any customer records in your free account. To remove the app from your POS too, uninstall Clicktide in your POS's app settings — the steps are shown on your report.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The audit and the report are free, with no credit card. If you then want Clicktide to win customers back, every plan starts with a 30-day free trial.

What happens after the report?

Nothing, unless you choose a plan. Plans are $79, $199 or $449 a month, each with a 30-day free trial. Checkout asks for a card, and if you cancel before day 30 you aren't charged. Your POS stays connected, so you don't have to set anything up again.

Which POS systems work?

Square, Shopify, Clover and Stripe connect directly. Using something else — Toast, Mindbody, Lightspeed, a spreadsheet? Upload a CSV export of your orders instead; names and contact details never leave your browser.

How does Clicktide decide a customer is "drifting"?

For anyone who has visited more than once, Clicktide learns their own average gap between visits and flags them once they're about 1.5× past it (between 14 and 120 days). One-time customers use a 60-day default. It's the same rule the Clicktide dashboard uses every day.

Is the "historical spend" number money I'll get back?

No. It's what those customers spent with you before they drifted — a measure of what's at stake, not a promise. The report keeps historical spend, an illustrative estimate, and actual recovered revenue as three separate numbers.

Will you contact my customers?

Not during the audit — nothing is sent. After subscribing, campaigns only run once you turn them on; texts only go to customers who've opted in, and every email has a working unsubscribe.

What if I don't have much history?

The report tells you plainly when there isn't enough data — for example, too few repeat customers to learn visit rhythms — rather than guessing.

How long does it take?

Usually two to five minutes, depending on how many orders your POS holds. You'll see real progress as it runs.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Your report has a Disconnect and delete my data button that deletes Clicktide's stored access to your POS, your report, and any customer records in your free account.
