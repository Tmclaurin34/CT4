Stage 1 · The ad page

They land from the ad

1

The offer, with an example report

The headline asks how much revenue they lose to customers who never come back. Beside it, an example report built from a sample café shows exactly what they'll get. Each ad gets its own matching headline.

2

The problem, and how Clicktide finds it

Regulars quietly stop coming and nobody notices. Clicktide reads purchase history, learns each customer's own rhythm, flags who's drifting and shows what they're worth.

3

How it works

Five plain steps: select your POS, connect securely, Clicktide analyzes, receive the report, then turn on win-backs only if you choose.

4

Security and privacy

What Clicktide reads, what it never does, and what happens to the data. Disconnecting is one click and deletes the stored access key, the report and any customer records.

Stage 2 · Connect

They sign up free and connect their POS

5

Free account

A progress bar shows the three steps. Business name, name, email and password, with a Show button on the password. No credit card. If the email already has an account, they go straight to log-in with it filled in.

6

Pick their POS

Square, Shopify, Clover or Stripe. They log in on their POS company's own site and come straight back. Shopify owners type their store address right on the page. Any other system can upload a CSV export instead.
